ℹ How to use this
Enter your domain to see all SSL certificates ever issued for it and its subdomains. Look for unexpected issuers (certs not from your CA), wildcard certs you didn't request, or certs for subdomains you don't own — these are all red flags for phishing infrastructure targeting your brand.
📨 Email Spoofing Vulnerability Test
Analyze if your domain can be spoofed
⚠ Educational Use Only. This tool analyzes your DNS records to determine if email spoofing is theoretically possible. It does not send any emails. Use this to identify and fix vulnerabilities before attackers exploit them.
Analyzing DNS records for spoofing vulnerabilities...
📚 What we check
SPF Policy
Whether the SPF record enforces rejection or allows spoofing via permissive policies like +all or missing records.
DMARC Enforcement
Whether DMARC is set to reject/quarantine or just monitoring mode (p=none), which allows spoofed mail through.
DKIM Signing
Whether outbound email is cryptographically signed, making it harder to forge convincingly.
Subdomain Policy
Whether the DMARC sp= tag protects subdomains from being used as spoofing vectors.
